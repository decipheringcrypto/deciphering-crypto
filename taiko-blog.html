Taiko – Ethereum Scaling with Zero Compromises

Published: June 15, 2025

Introduction

Taiko is an ambitious Layer-2 project bringing scalability to Ethereum without compromising decentralization or compatibility...

What Problem Does Taiko Solve?

Ethereum’s biggest bottlenecks have long been high gas fees and limited throughput...

Top 3 Standout Features

1. Ethereum Equivalence

Taiko is a Type-1 ZK-EVM — meaning it doesn’t change anything about Ethereum’s execution layer...

2. Decentralized Sequencing

Unlike many rollups that rely on a centralized sequencer... Taiko uses Ethereum’s validators to determine transaction order...

3. Future-Proof Architecture

Taiko is built with modularity in mind and supports evolving zk-proof systems...

Security: Contestable Proofs + Ethereum Finality

Taiko implements a game-theoretic contestation mechanism for bad proofs and uses Ethereum for finality...

User Experience

Users connect their MetaMask, bridge ETH, and interact with dApps just like they would on Ethereum...
